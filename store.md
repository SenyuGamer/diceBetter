# 🎲 Better Dice

**Better Dice** is a high-performance, physics-based 3D dice roller designed for Owlbear Rodeo. It guarantees deterministic, synchronized dice rolls for the entire party, making sure every natural 20 is shared and celebrated in real-time.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Physics-Based Rolling**: Powered by the Rapier physics engine, ensuring truly random and deeply satisfying 3D dice physics synchronized perfectly across all players' screens.
- **D&D Beyond Integration**: Rolls can be triggered directly from your D&D Beyond character sheet using our companion browser extension, **BeyondOwl**.
- **Global & Local History**: Keep a transparent record of the party's luck with a synchronized global history log, while maintaining a personal log for your own rolls.
- **Advanced Mechanics**: Full support for Advantage, Disadvantage, hidden rolls (whisper to GM), and mechanical modifiers like *Bless* or *Bane*.
- **Quick-Access Saved Rolls**: Save your most frequently used attacks and spell damage macros directly into the UI for one-click rolling.
- **Accessible & Responsive**: Fully supports mobile devices, iPads, and adapts automatically to Owlbear Rodeo's Light and Dark themes.

## 🌉 D&D Beyond Integration (BeyondOwl)

By installing the **BeyondOwl** browser companion extension, you can turn your D&D Beyond character sheet into a powerful control panel. Clicking any weapon attack, skill check, or saving throw on D&D Beyond will instantly drop the corresponding 3D dice directly into your Owlbear Rodeo room!

[🦊 Get BeyondOwl for Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/es-ES/android/addon/beyondowl/)

*(Chrome Web Store release coming soon).*

## 📖 How to Use

1. **Install the Extension**: Copy the manifest link and add it to your Owlbear Rodeo room.
2. **Open the Tray**: Click the Better Dice icon in the toolbar to open the 3D dice tray.
3. **Roll**: Select the dice you want to roll, add any bonuses, and click the roll button!
4. **Save Macros**: Use the Star icon to save a specific dice combination for future sessions.
