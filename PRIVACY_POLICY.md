# Privacy Policy for BeyondOwl & Better Dice

**Last Updated:** September 2026

We deeply respect your privacy. This policy outlines how the BeyondOwl browser extension and the Better Dice Owlbear Rodeo extension handle data.

## 1. Data Collection & Usage
**We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**
- **BeyondOwl (Browser Extension):** Operates strictly locally. It reads character data and roll modifiers directly from your active D&D Beyond tab and forwards them securely to your active Owlbear Rodeo tab using native browser messaging APIs. No data is ever sent to an external server, database, or third-party service.
- **Better Dice (Owlbear Rodeo Extension):** Operates entirely within the Owlbear Rodeo infrastructure. Dice rolls and history are synchronized across players using Owlbear Rodeo's official native SDK (`OBR.broadcast`). We do not host any external servers that process or monitor your rolls.

## 2. Analytics and Tracking
We do **not** use any analytics software, trackers, or telemetry in either of the extensions. We do not track your browsing history or your usage patterns.

## 3. Storage
Any settings you configure (such as saved rolls, UI preferences, or connected tabs) are saved directly in your browser's local storage (via `localStorage` or `chrome.storage.local`). We have no access to this data.

## 4. Third-Party Access
Since we do not collect any data, we cannot and do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to third parties.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or the extensions' source code, please feel free to open an issue on our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/SenyuGamer/diceBetter).
